# A New World — Release Notes v1.0

**A New World adds 40 playable civilizations to Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition, featuring historical leaders from the Americas, Africa, Asia, and revolutionary Europe. Play as Pachacuti's Inca Empire in the Andes, lead Montezuma's Aztec confederacy in Mesoamerica, command Wellington's British forces or Hidalgo's rising Mexican nation, command Crazy Horse's Lakota resistance, or guide Napoleon across Europe. Every civilization arrives with its own home city, curated 25-card deck, AI personality, and leader doctrine that scales from Discovery to Imperial Age.**

## What's New in v1.0

- **40 fully playable civilizations** available in Skirmish and single-player from the lobby picker
- **Smart AI wall placement** — chokepoint detection, water-aware positioning, and automatic gap closure
- **Per-civ build-order personalities** — each leader favors different strategies (naval mercantile, highland fortress, jungle guerrilla, nomadic raiding, etc.)
- **Home City & Deck integration** — every civ has a historically styled home city and matching 25-card deck
- **In-game visual verification** — diplomat panel flag clicks to inspect AI home cities and decks for gameplay consistency

## Installation & Play

1. Subscribe to this mod in the Steam Workshop
2. Launch Age of Empires III: Definitive Edition
3. Select **Skirmish** or campaign
4. In the lobby, choose any A New World civilization from the picker
5. Select your leader (each civ has one historical figure with a unique home city and deck)
6. Start the match — AI opponents will use their full personalities
