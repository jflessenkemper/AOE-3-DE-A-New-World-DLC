Louis-Joseph Papineau (1786-1871) led the Patriote movement in Lower Canada, demanding responsible government from the British colonial administration in the 1830s. The Province of Canada (1841-1867) merged Upper and Lower Canada after the 1837-1838 rebellions, paving the road toward Confederation and the Dominion of Canada.
