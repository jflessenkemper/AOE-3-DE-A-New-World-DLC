Bernardo O'Higgins (1778-1842) co-led the Andean campaign with San Martín and served as Chile's Supreme Director (1817-1823), instituting the new republic's military and constitution. The Republic of Chile defended itself from royalist counter-attacks and, in the 1879-83 War of the Pacific, seized the nitrate-rich Atacama from Bolivia and Peru.
